# Prioritization and Decision Readiness

Catalyst Canvas v1.8.0 compares alternatives without presenting rankings as objective truth.

## Methods

The shared engine supports:

- ICE: impact × confidence × ease
- RICE: reach × impact × confidence ÷ effort
- editable weighted criteria
- impact–effort matrix
- confidence–risk matrix
- urgency–importance matrix
- reversibility and commitment matrix
- sensitivity scenarios
- ethical and governance gates

Definitions and scales remain editable. Each value records whether it is measured, estimated, opinion-based, or unknown, along with confidence, rationale, and evidence or assumption links.

## Recommendation states

- explore
- test
- defer
- reject
- escalate
- ready for decision review

These states describe workflow position. They are not approvals.

## Sensitivity analysis

A sensitivity scenario contains weight overrides and a newly calculated ranking. Baseline weights and raw criterion values remain unchanged. Rank deltas show where the scenario changes the comparison.

## Decision handoffs

`catalyst-canvas-decision-handoff/1.0` packages preserve:

- alternatives and all scoring inputs;
- criteria and sensitivity views;
- assumptions and evidence;
- unresolved research questions;
- decision notes and handoff records;
- Canvas and revision provenance.

Decision Studio receives governance questions and recommendation states. Workbench receives calculation requirements, modeling questions, and all ICE/RICE score inputs for technical validation.
