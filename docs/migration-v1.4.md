# Canvas Migration to Contract 1.4

Catalyst Canvas v1.7.0 recognizes Canvas Contracts 1.0, 1.1, 1.2, and 1.3, plus supported legacy Python, wrapper, and Flask exports.

Recognized records pass through the canonical migration and normalization engine. The migrated document declares `catalyst-canvas/1.4`, records `provenance.migrated_from`, and preserves a warning that research, ideation, and prioritization fields may require review.

Contract 1.3 projects retain their identities, research records, ledger records, custom frameworks, prompt packs, sessions, ideas, clusters, prototypes, tests, and revision provenance. Missing decision records are initialized through the shared criteria and prioritization model.

Unknown future contract versions are rejected.
