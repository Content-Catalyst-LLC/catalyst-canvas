# Catalyst Canvas v1.7.0

## Prioritization and Decision Readiness

This release introduces Canvas Contract 1.4 and adds a transparent decision layer after research and ideation.

Highlights:

- editable criterion library and weights;
- ICE, RICE, and weighted scoring with visible basis, confidence, rationale, evidence, and assumptions;
- four matrix views;
- ethical and governance gates;
- sensitivity scenarios that preserve raw values;
- dependencies, blockers, resources, owners, and deadlines;
- recommendation states and decision notes;
- Decision Studio and Workbench handoffs;
- matching Python, Flask, and WordPress browser engines.

Scores support comparison. They do not make decisions or resolve uncertainty.
