# Catalyst Canvas v1.5.0

## Research, Evidence, and Assumption Ledger

Version 1.5.0 adds the governed research and evidence layer required before Catalyst Canvas advances into broader ideation, prioritization, and experimentation releases.

### Delivered

- Canvas Contract 1.2 and Contract 1.0/1.1 migration
- source and evidence registers
- claim states, uncertainty, contradiction, limitation, missing-data, and review fields
- assumption ownership, criticality, consequence, test method, status, due date, evidence, and experiment links
- research questions, interview guides, observation notes, and synthesis tags
- descriptive ledger coverage and exposure indicators
- Flask Evidence Ledger workspace
- reusable workspace ledger assets
- Knowledge Library and Research Librarian handoff packages
- publication warnings for unsupported, disputed, and outdated claims
- browser-local WordPress ledger and indicators
- deterministic Python, Flask, and Node Contract 1.2 conformance

### Compatibility

Existing v1.4 projects remain readable. Contract 1.1 records migrate to 1.2 on normalization, import, save, or reuse. Existing v1.3/v1.4 SQLite databases are retained by the installer and upgraded idempotently when the application initializes.

### Safety and governance boundary

The ledger makes provenance and uncertainty inspectable. It does not automatically validate sources, resolve contradictions, establish causality, or approve claims for publication.
