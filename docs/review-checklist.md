# Canvas Review Checklist

Before a Canvas is used for a decision, prototype, or publication, confirm:

- the document declares `catalyst-canvas/1.1` and has stable Canvas and revision IDs;
- the primary audience, affected stakeholders, and exclusions are explicit;
- persona attributes identify source type, confidence, and validation status;
- accessibility needs and potentially sensitive attributes are handled carefully;
- stakeholder influence, interest, stance, and decision role are current and evidence-aware;
- journey stages reflect actual or explicitly provisional behavior rather than invented certainty;
- constraints, evidence gaps, and critical assumptions remain visible;
- evidence and assumption IDs are linked where appropriate;
- prototypes and tests define a learning signal;
- review notes identify overclaiming, representation, or interpretation risks;
- provenance identifies the source surface and migration warnings.
