# Review Checklist

Before treating a Canvas as ready for broader review, confirm that:

- the document declares `catalyst-canvas/2.0` and has stable Canvas and revision IDs;
- material claims expose supporting evidence, contradictions, limitations, and review state;
- high-criticality assumptions have an owner and test method;
- idea and decision lineage remains traceable to the challenge and evidence;
- scoring inputs preserve basis, confidence, rationale, and unknown values;
- failed or unknown gates remain visible;
- prototypes record version, owner, limitations, and success definition;
- hypotheses are falsifiable and linked to the relevant prototype or assumption;
- experiment plans define participants, metrics, thresholds, safeguards, and stop conditions;
- result records distinguish observed values from interpretation;
- learning decisions and iterations cite the runs and evidence that caused them;
- handoff packages include the correct target-specific context and provenance.

Readiness indicators describe workflow coverage. They do not establish truth, approval, causality, safety, or implementation success.
