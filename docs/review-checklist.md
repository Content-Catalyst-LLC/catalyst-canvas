# Canvas Review Checklist

Before a Canvas is used for a decision, prototype, handoff, or publication, confirm:

- the document declares `catalyst-canvas/1.3` and has stable Canvas and revision IDs;
- the primary audience, affected stakeholders, and exclusions are explicit;
- material sources have usable provenance, rights, limitations, and ownership notes where applicable;
- evidence excerpts preserve source links, locators, citations, confidence, and limitations;
- every material claim has an explicit state and identifies evidence, uncertainty, contradictions, missing data, and review status;
- unsupported, disputed, and outdated claims are not presented as established findings;
- high-criticality assumptions have an owner, consequence, test method, status, and experiment link;
- open research questions remain visible rather than being converted into premature conclusions;
- persona attributes identify basis, evidence, confidence, and validation status;
- accessibility needs and potentially sensitive attributes are handled carefully;
- stakeholder influence, interest, impact, stance, and decision role are current and evidence-aware;
- journey stages reflect observed or explicitly provisional behavior;
- prototypes and tests define a learning signal;
- review notes identify overclaiming, representation, or interpretation risks;
- handoffs identify their target, purpose, included records, and provenance;
- provenance identifies the source surface and migration warnings.
